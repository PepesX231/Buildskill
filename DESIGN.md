# Project Build Skill: illustrated course page

The page has five sections, in the owner's requested order. Keep the frameless navigation, readable text, no decorative section indices, no stars, no English eyebrow filler, and no arrow suffixes on controls.

1. Hero: the actual course name and offer, one short outcome, and an original chibi creator scene with the orange-white hamster. The illustration explains the course; it is not presented as a real student's work.
2. Roadmap: three generated story scenes connected by the curved journey the owner requested. Selecting a phase reveals a short list; selecting game/app/IoT changes the relevant example and enrollment preference.
3. Comparison: a blue editorial section with a mentorship illustration and four meaningful comparison rows. Self-learning remains a valid route.
4. Real work: screenshots from the linked projects, developer attribution, concise context, and direct links. A second tab connects real projects to the community members' portfolio stories.
5. Package: price and dates, a celebratory project-and-portfolio illustration, four concise inclusion lines, and expandable practical questions. No invented lesson times, prize guarantees, refund terms, or payment plans.

Art direction: original chibi characters with oversized heads, graphic silhouettes, dark plum hair, ivory/cobalt/coral styling, and the hamster. Inspired by the owner's supplied visual reference; no reference poster lettering, signatures, or credits are copied.

Palette: ink #261d31, hero #0c0d16, cream #fff8ef, cobalt #277fe8, coral/pink #ffe0e7, orange #f79b45. Body text is generally 15–17px; headings are 34–54px. Images are supplemental to short, readable copy.

Generated PNG illustrations are converted to WebP with transparency for delivery. Real proof images retain their original appearance and link back to their project pages.
